Features That Make a Difference

No forgotten passwords. Employees verify with their name and date of birth for simple, secure access.

Attendly automatically calculates the total hours worked (e.g., 8.50 hours) as soon as an employee punches out.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to run Attendly?

All you need is a standard, free Google account. The system runs entirely on Google Sheets and Google Apps Script.

Is it difficult to install?

Not at all! Our Starter package comes with a detailed, step-by-step guide that will have you up and running in about 15-20 minutes. If you want us to handle everything, our Business package includes a complete white-glove installation service.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. Unlike other SaaS products, your data never passes through our servers. It stays securely within your own Google account, protected by Google's world-class security.

Can I customize the system?

Yes. The code is fully accessible, allowing you or your developer to customize it. Our Business package includes adding your company name and logo for you.

What if I have more than 50 employees?

The system is built on Google Sheets, which can handle thousands of rows of data without issue, making it suitable for growing businesses.
